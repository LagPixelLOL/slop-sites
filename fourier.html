Painting multiplies the frequency magnitude (0x = erase, 1x = restore original, up to 10x = boost). Edits are mirrored to keep the image real. All mode edits every channel; R / G / B edit one. Output: Magnitude shows |pixel| after the inverse FFT (negatives fold bright, no contrast stretch); Real clamps negatives to black.
